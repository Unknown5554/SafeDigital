BE A RESPONSIBLE DIGITAL CITIZEN

Before you post,
run a quick check.

You don't need to be an expert to protect someone's privacy. Start with a few thoughtful habits.

✓
Get clear permission

Ask the person before posting their photo, video, or private message.

✓
Think about the impact

Could this embarrass, expose, or put someone at risk?

✓
Keep private things private

Don't reveal personal details or forward private conversations without consent.

✓
Choose a helpful response

If you see cyberbullying, don't join in. Report it and support the person affected.
